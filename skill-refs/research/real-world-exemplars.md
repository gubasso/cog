# Real-world exemplars

Single source of truth for the real-world reference-implementation research directive. Injected into the `ask -r` research sub-prompt by both `ask` twins, resolved in their own context via `cog skill-refs path research/real-world-exemplars.md`.

## Directive

Perform deep web research into real-world reference implementations and the projects widely regarded as best-in-class at solving similar problems. Surface the best-established patterns, practices, architectures, and coding designs for this kind of problem, name the exemplar projects you find, and explain why each is exemplary (proven at scale, idiomatic for the stack, well-documented, widely adopted). Cite the projects and sources relied on.
